# 🛍️ Project Definition Document  
### **Made in Cameroon Marketplace**

---

## 📌 Project Concept

**Made in Cameroon Marketplace** is a digital platform exclusively dedicated to showcasing, selling, and promoting products crafted, grown, or assembled in Cameroon. The platform aims to bridge the digital divide faced by local producers and artisans by providing:

- An **e-commerce marketplace** tailored for Cameroonian vendors.
- Seamless **integration with local payment systems** (MTN Mobile Money, Orange Money).
- A mobile-first, multilingual experience for inclusive access.

This platform is built with a deep respect for **cultural pride**, **local economic empowerment**, and **digital inclusion**.

---

## 🎯 Project Goals

### 💼 Economic Empowerment
- Enable local artisans, farmers, and small businesses to access national and international buyers.
- Reduce reliance on physical markets and middlemen.
- Retain value within the local economy.

### 🌐 Digital Inclusion
- Provide a **mobile-first, user-friendly interface** for low-literacy and low-connectivity regions.
- Ensure the platform is accessible in **English, French, and local languages**.

### 💳 Payment Integration
- Fully integrate **MTN Mobile Money and Orange Money** to reflect local payment behavior.
- Eliminate barriers for the unbanked population.

### 👥 Community & Culture
- Promote **Cameroonian identity** and support for “Buy Local” movements.
- Foster a community of sellers and buyers who value authenticity and trust.

---

## 🚧 Key Challenges

| Challenge                        | Description                                                                 |
|----------------------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Limited Digital Skills**       | Many potential sellers may lack experience with digital platforms.          |
| **Mobile Money Integration**     | APIs from MTN and Orange may pose challenges in access, testing, or docs.   |
| **Platform Trust & Safety**      | Ensuring buyers feel secure and sellers operate ethically.                  |
| **Logistics**                    | Delivery and pickup models for rural and urban areas need to be well defined. |
| **Platform Adoption**            | Convincing vendors to join and remain engaged requires strong onboarding.   |
| **Cultural Relevance**           | The UX/UI must resonate with local users across different languages and devices. |

---

## 🌟 Strategic Opportunity

- Rising **national pride and demand** for local products.
- **Mobile money dominance** in daily transactions across Cameroon.
- **Lack of a marketplace** focused exclusively on local products — *a clear gap*.
- Support from the **diaspora** and impact-focused consumers globally.
